# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 May 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how quanhook ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the quanhook mobile application and related services.

By using quanhook you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

## Who we are

quanhook is operated by devls.tech ("we"). You can reach us at **support@quanhook.app**.

## What we collect

We collect only what is needed to make the app work. Specifically:

### Information you give us
- **Account information** — your email address, name, and a password (or, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the identifier they pass us). Used to create and secure your account.
- **Profile content** — anything you choose to add to your profile (display name, avatar, bio).
- **User-generated content** — places you Hook (save), Skip, or Ignore; reviews you write (headline, body text, sentiment, photos); comments, replies, and reactions you post; photos you attach to a review or your profile; Scenes (mini-itineraries) you compose; and the people you follow.

### Information collected automatically
- **Location** — when you grant permission, we use your device's precise location to show nearby places and personalize recommendations. Location is collected only while you are using the app ("when in use"). You can revoke this at any time in your device settings; the app will still work using a city you pick manually.
- **Device and usage data** — basic information about your device (model, OS version, app version, language) and crash diagnostics, to keep the app stable.
- **Identifiers** — a quanhook user ID and a device-level identifier for authentication and abuse prevention.

### Information you let us access
- **Photos** — when you attach an image to a review or your profile, we read that single photo from your library. We do not scan or upload other photos.
- **Camera** — only when you tap to take a new photo from within the app.

We do **not** collect contacts, microphone audio, health data, financial data, or precise location in the background.

## How we use your information

- To operate the core features of quanhook (recommending places, running tag-based AI searches, showing reviews, composing Scenes, following other users).
- To personalize what you see (nearby places, suggestions based on your location and history).
- To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
- To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.
- To communicate important account or service notices.
- To comply with law and to investigate fraud, abuse, or violations of our terms.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

## How information is shared

We share information only with parties that help us run the service:

- **Cloud hosting and storage** — to store your account, content, and uploaded photos.
- **Identity providers** (Google, Apple) — only if you choose to sign in with them.
- **Map data providers** — map tiles are rendered from MapLibre-compatible sources; only the map view bounds (not your identity) are sent in those requests.
- **Crash and performance monitoring** — anonymized device and error information may be sent to a diagnostics provider (such as Sentry) so we can fix bugs.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the safety of users.

Content you choose to make public — for example, a review you post or a scene you share via link — is visible to other quanhook users and to anyone with the share link.

## Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are legally required to keep certain records (for example, transaction logs).

## Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account directly from the app: open **Settings → Delete account**. This removes your profile, your Hooks, Skips, and Ignores, your reviews, comments, replies and reactions, your Scenes, and any uploaded photos. Public content you posted will be removed or anonymized.

You may also email **support@quanhook.app** and we will process the deletion within 30 days.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, UK, or California), you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you,
- correct inaccurate information,
- request deletion,
- object to or restrict certain processing,
- request a portable copy of your data,
- withdraw consent you previously gave.

To exercise any of these, email **support@quanhook.app**. We will respond within 30 days.

## Children

quanhook is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## User-generated content and moderation

quanhook hosts content created by its users (reviews, memories, scenes, profiles). To report objectionable content or a user, email **support@quanhook.app** with a link or screenshot. Reports are reviewed and acted on — typically within 24 hours — if they violate our community guidelines. In-app reporting and blocking controls are being added in an upcoming release.

## International transfers

quanhook is operated from outside the country where you may live. By using the app, you understand that your information may be processed in countries with different data-protection laws than your own. We take appropriate steps (contractual safeguards and access controls) to protect it wherever it is processed.

## Security

We protect your information with industry-standard measures: TLS for all network traffic, encrypted secure storage on the device for authentication tokens, hashed passwords, and least-privilege access to production data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and let us know immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we will notify you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent revision.
