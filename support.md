# quanhook Support

Need help with quanhook? You're in the right place.

The fastest way to reach us is email — we usually respond within one business day.

**support@quanhook.app**

---

## Common questions

### How do I sign in?
Open quanhook, tap **Sign in** on the welcome screen, and choose email, Google, or Apple. If you forgot your password, tap **Forgot password?** on the email sign-in screen and follow the link we email you.

### Why doesn't quanhook show places near me?
quanhook needs your location to find nearby places. If you didn't grant location permission, open your device **Settings → quanhook → Location** and allow it "While Using the App". Alternatively, tap the location chip at the top of the Home tab and pick a city manually.

### How do I save a memory of a place I visited?
Open the place's detail screen, scroll to **Memories**, and tap **Add memory**. You can attach a photo from your library or take a new one with the camera, add a short note, and rate the visit.

### What is a "Scene"?
A Scene is a small itinerary that quanhook composes for you. Open the **Scenes** tab, describe what you want ("rainy-afternoon cafés in District 3", "a slow Sunday walk near the river"), and tap submit. quanhook streams a list of stops you can save, tweak, or share with friends.

### How do I share a Scene?
Open the Scene, tap the share icon, and either copy the link or share it through your usual apps. Anyone with the link can view the Scene; they don't need a quanhook account.

### How do I report a review, profile, or photo?
Email **support@quanhook.app** with a link to the content (or a screenshot) and a short note about what's wrong. We review reports — typically within 24 hours — and take action if the content violates our community guidelines. In-app reporting controls are coming in a future update.

### How do I block another user?
In-app blocking is being added in a future update. In the meantime, email **support@quanhook.app** with the user's profile link and we'll act on it manually.

### How do I change the language?
quanhook supports English and Vietnamese. Open the **Settings** tab; the language selector is at the top of the screen.

### How do I delete my account?
Open **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently removes your profile, memories, reviews, scenes, and uploaded photos. Public content will be removed or anonymized. The action cannot be undone.

If you'd prefer to have us delete the account for you, email **support@quanhook.app** from the email address on the account and we'll process the request within 30 days.

### Does quanhook work offline?
Most browsing requires an internet connection. Memories you have already viewed are cached, but creating new memories, posting reviews, and composing Scenes need a connection.

### Is quanhook free?
Yes. The core app is free to use. We may introduce paid features in the future; if we do, they'll be clearly labeled and entirely optional.

## Bug reports and feature requests

If you found a bug or have an idea, email **support@quanhook.app** with:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead
- Your device model and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18.2)
- The quanhook app version (Settings → About)
- A screenshot or screen recording if you can

We read every message.
