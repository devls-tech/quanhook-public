# quanhook Support

Need help with quanhook? You're in the right place.

The fastest way to reach us is email — we usually respond within one business day.

**support@quanhook.app**

---

## Getting started

### How do I sign in?
Open quanhook, tap **Sign in** on the welcome screen, and choose **Continue with Google** or **email**. If you sign up with email, we send a one-time password to your inbox — enter it on the next screen to finish signing in. The password expires in 10 minutes; if it doesn't arrive, tap **Didn't get it? Resend**.

### I forgot my password.
On the email sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and we'll send you a one-time password you can use to sign in. The code expires in 10 minutes.

### Which languages does quanhook support?
English and Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt). To switch, open the **Settings** tab — the language selector is at the top of the screen.

## Discovering places (Home tab)

### Why aren't any places showing up?
The Home tab needs your location to recommend places near you. If you didn't grant location permission, open your device **Settings → quanhook → Location** and allow it "While Using the App". You can also tap the **location chip** at the top of the Home tab and pick a city manually — that becomes the source of truth until you change it.

### What do the buttons under each place do?
- **Heart (Hook)** — save this place to your hooked list and tell us you like the recommendation.
- **X (Skip)** — pass on this place; we won't show it to you again.
- **Undo (curved arrow)** — undo your last Hook or Skip.
- **i (Details)** — open the full place screen with photos, tags, sub-ratings, and reviews.
- **Share** — copy or share a link to this place.

### Why does the deck say "No places found near you"?
There aren't enough indexed places at your exact coordinates yet. quanhook will fall back to showing popular places from a wider area. Try picking a nearby major city from the location chip.

## Searching with AI (Search tab)

### How does Search work?
quanhook's Search tab is tag-driven. Add a few tags that describe what you're looking for ("rooftop", "cozy", "vegan", "rainy day"), pick the city or use your current location, and tap **Search**. quanhook returns nearby matches and then an AI agent enriches each result with a short reason for why it fits — you'll see the **Researching** progress banner while it works.

### Where did the AI suggestions for tags come from?
As you add tags, quanhook proposes related ones based on what you've already picked. They're suggestions only — feel free to ignore them.

### Can I see results on a map?
Yes. On the results screen, tap the **map icon** in the top right to switch between list and map views. Pins are clustered when you zoom out.

### Can I see my past searches?
Tap **History** in the top right of the Search tab. Each entry shows the tags you used, the city, and how many places we found. Tap one to re-open it.

## Reviews

### Where do reviews come from?
The Reviews tab is a community feed of reviews about places listed in quanhook. You can sort by **Recent**, **Popular**, or **Trending**.

### What can I do with a review?
Tap a review to open it. From there you can **like** or **dislike** it, **comment**, and tap through to the place it's about. Like, dislike, and comment actions require an account.

### Can I write a review from the mobile app?
Not yet — writing new reviews from mobile is on the roadmap. For now, the mobile app is focused on reading reviews and engaging with them via comments and reactions.

### How do I report a review, comment, profile, or photo?
Email **support@quanhook.app** with a link to the content (or a screenshot) and a short note about what's wrong. We review reports — typically within 24 hours — and take action if the content violates our community guidelines. In-app reporting controls are coming in a future update.

### How do I block another user?
In-app blocking is being added in a future update. In the meantime, email **support@quanhook.app** with the user's profile link and we'll handle it manually.

## Scenes

### What is a Scene?
A Scene is a small itinerary that quanhook composes for you. Open the **Scenes** tab, describe what you want ("a slow Sunday walk near the river", "rainy-afternoon cafés in District 3"), and submit. quanhook streams a list of stops, one by one, that you can save and tweak.

### How do I find my saved Scenes?
On the Scenes tab, tap **My scenes** in the top right.

### How do I share a Scene?
Open the Scene and tap the share icon. Anyone with the link can view it without a quanhook account.

## Profile and following

### Can I follow other users?
Yes. Open someone's profile (tap their avatar or name from a review or comment) and tap **Follow**. You can see who you follow and who follows you from your own profile.

### How do I edit my profile?
Open the **Profile** tab and tap **Edit**. You can change your display name, bio, and avatar.

## Notifications

### What are notifications for?
We use them to let you know about things involving your activity — for example, when someone comments on a review you commented on, follows you, or shares a Scene with you. Tap the bell on the Home tab, or pull up the **Notifications** screen, to see them.

### How do I turn notifications off?
Open **Settings → Notifications** to manage what we send you. You can also disable them entirely in your device's system settings.

## Account and data

### How do I delete my account?
Open **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently removes your profile, your hooks/skips, comments and reactions, and uploaded photos. Public content will be removed or anonymized. The action cannot be undone.

If you'd prefer to have us delete the account for you, email **support@quanhook.app** from the email address on the account and we'll process the request within 30 days.

### How do I change my password?
Open **Settings → Change password**.

### Does quanhook work offline?
Most browsing requires an internet connection. Some content you've already seen is cached, but searching, opening place details for the first time, posting comments, and composing Scenes all need a connection.

### Is quanhook free?
Yes. The core app is free to use. We may introduce paid features in the future; if we do, they'll be clearly labeled and entirely optional.

## Bug reports and feature requests

If you found a bug or have an idea, email **support@quanhook.app** with:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead
- Your device model and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18.2)
- The quanhook app version (Settings → About)
- A screenshot or screen recording if you can

We read every message.
