# quanhook

quanhook is an app for discovering great places near you, reading what others think about them, and composing small AI-curated itineraries you can share with friends.

quanhook is in beta and currently live in Ho Chi Minh City. The app itself lives at [quanhook.com](https://quanhook.com); this page hosts the resources quanhook users and the App Store / Play Store need:

- [Privacy Policy](privacy)
- [Support](support)

For anything else, email **support@quanhook.app**.
