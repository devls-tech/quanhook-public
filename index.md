# quanhook

quanhook is a mobile app for discovering great places, capturing memories from the ones you visit, and composing small itineraries you can share with friends.

The main [quanhook.com](https://quanhook.com) site is still under construction. In the meantime, this page hosts the resources quanhook users and the App Store / Play Store need:

- [Privacy Policy](privacy)
- [Support](support)

For anything else, email **support@quanhook.app**.
